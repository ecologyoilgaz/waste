4. Short-Term Solutions

  • Waste-to-energy plant — reduce landfilling. Cost: ~$100M.
  • Landfill degassing — capture methane. Cost: ~$20M.
  • Sorting infrastructure — reduce volume. Cost: ~$30M.
Total investment: ~$150M. Expected benefit: extended landfill life, reduced methane, and energy recovery.
